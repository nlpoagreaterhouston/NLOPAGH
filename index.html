About Our Chapter

Dedicated to Serving Houston with Integrity and Heart

Founded in the fall of 2014 by dedicated officers from the Houston Independent School District Police Department and surrounding regional agencies, the NLPOA Greater Houston Chapter is part of the largest and most respected Latino law enforcement organization in the United States.

Our core mission is to provide career training, promote education and advancement, eliminate prejudice and discrimination within the criminal justice system, and foster mutual trust between law enforcement and the vibrant communities of Greater Houston. Membership is warmly open to all peace officers, criminal justice professionals, and community supporters regardless of race or background.

Meet Our Executive Board

1. Education

Empowering the next generation through our Annual Scholarship Fund, helping Houston-area high school graduates and college students achieve their dreams in higher education and criminal justice careers.

2. Mentorship

Guiding future peace officers and community youth through structured career readiness workshops, leadership panels, and junior cadet development programs that build positive life skills.

3. Community & Justice

Strengthening trust, eliminating discrimination in the justice system, and serving vulnerable families through back-to-school backpack drives, Thanksgiving meals, and holiday toy distributions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the NLPOA Greater Houston Chapter?

Membership is open to all active, reserve, and retired law enforcement officers, criminal justice professionals, university/academy students, and community allies regardless of ethnic background.

How are scholarship funds distributed?

All scholarship funds are raised directly through our Annual Golf Tournament, corporate donations, and community galas. Checks are issued directly to accredited colleges and universities on behalf of the winning recipients.

Are contributions to NLPOA tax-deductible?

Yes! NLPOA is an established non-profit organization. Donations, hole sponsorships, and corporate gifts qualify for federal tax deductions to the extent permitted by law.
